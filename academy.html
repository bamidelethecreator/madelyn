Welcome to Madelyn Fashion Academy

Our academy is committed to helping aspiring fashion designers develop practical skills through structured instruction, creative exploration and hands-on learning. Whether you are just starting or looking to refine your abilities, there is a learning path for you.

Your Creative Journey

Our Training Levels

Our programmes are structured to support students at different stages of their fashion design journey.

Beginner Level

Build a strong foundation in sewing and fashion design.

  • Introduction to sewing tools
  • Fabric identification
  • Basic hand stitching
  • Introduction to machine sewing
  • Basic garment construction
  • Simple clothing projects

Intermediate Level

Strengthen your skills and develop your garment construction techniques.

  • Body measurements
  • Pattern drafting
  • Skirt construction
  • Trouser construction
  • Blouse and dress making
  • Garment finishing techniques

Advanced Level

Explore professional techniques and more complex fashion designs.

  • Advanced pattern drafting
  • Structured garment construction
  • Evening wear
  • Advanced finishing techniques
  • Bridal design fundamentals
  • Professional design projects
